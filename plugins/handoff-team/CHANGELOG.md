# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- **Hires can't be named Claude, Agents or Gemini.** Those names would make a job page such as `Team/CLAUDE.md`, which AI tools read as orders for every session in `Team/`. The card says the name is taken and shows no Hire button; the tool gate already refused these file names.

## 1.0.0

First release. The member's AI team office: a Chief of Staff and helpers that work from a second brain of plain notes, shown in a friendly Team View.

- **Start from an empty folder.** Team View offers **Make my brain** or **Practice brain** (a made-up business to try things on). The brain template ships with the add-on: job pages, job cards, trainings, the brain's rules and its guard.
- **Guided setup, one screen at a time:** backup to a private GitHub copy (Apple's tools, GitHub's helper, a sign-in code, the copy pinned), connected apps approved inside setup, the About My Business interview, the Chief's job page, the first brief, the cloud morning shift (steps copied, claude.ai's page opened), chimes. Setup resumes where the owner left off. A brain copied from the GitHub template is offered **Use your cloud copy** only when that copy is private and the owner's own.
- **Drafts only.** Employees never send, reply, forward, invite or post; messages become Gmail drafts the owner sends. Send buttons exist only when a brain turns on `sendFromTeamView`, and the setting is read fresh before every send.
- **The gate.** In a brain, employees read anything and write only working notes; never owner files, rules, trainings, `.claude`, `.handoff`, instruction files (CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, GEMINI.md and variants, look-alike letters folded) or names with hidden characters. Apps are used only after the owner OKs them. While Team View offers to make a brain, only reading goes on.
- **Cards the owner approves:** hires and trainings are kept only after the owner has seen every word, never with hidden characters, never under a name in use, and only if the note still matches what was shown.
- **Safety update** brings newer rules, job cards and built-in trainings into an existing brain on the owner's tap; never job pages or notes, never through a link.
- **Friendly Mode:** Claude's own screen in the same plain words; live activity lines; the energy meter; backup in plain words with "two versions" choices; opt-in chimes.
- **Cloud shifts:** the brain's committed guard keeps the same rules in Anthropic's cloud, saves only to the brain's own private copy, and lets the shift send its summary to the owner's phone.
